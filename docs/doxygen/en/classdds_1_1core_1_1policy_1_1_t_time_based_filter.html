Detailed Description

template<typename D>
class dds::core::policy::TTimeBasedFilter< D >

Sets the minimum separation between samples selected for a reader.

The separation applies independently to each keyed instance. A zero separation applies no time-based filtering. Use a positive duration when the application needs updates less frequently than the writer publishes them.

Set the reader's minimum separation to a duration less than or equal to its dds::core::policy::TDeadline period. A larger separation makes the two policies inconsistent.

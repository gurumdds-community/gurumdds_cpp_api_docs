Detailed Description

template<typename D>
class dds::core::policy::TResourceLimits< D >

Bounds the samples and instances that an entity can maintain.

max_samples() limits the total samples held across instances. max_instances() limits the number of distinct instances. max_samples_per_instance() limits samples for one key. Use dds_LENGTH_UNLIMITED for a limit without an explicit bound.

The total sample limit must accommodate the per-instance sample limit. With KEEP_LAST history, the history depth must also fit within the per-instance sample limit. KEEP_ALL remains subject to these limits.
